06 · BUYER'S CHECKLIST

What to check before you buy.

Ten minutes with the car tells you more than any ad. Bring a flashlight and a paper towel - here is where to point them.

Cold start & idle

Ask for a cold start. The needle spikes, then should settle near 750 rpm and hold dead steady. A hunting or bouncing idle means vacuum leaks or worse.

Red flag: ticking or rattle that stays after 30 seconds.
Oil leaks

Look under the engine and along the valve cover with your flashlight. A dusty oil film is normal at this age - fresh, wet drips are not.

Red flag: fresh drops on the ground where it was parked.
BLUE = BURNING OIL
Exhaust smoke

A puff of vapour at cold start is fine. Steady blue smoke means burning oil, sweet-smelling white means coolant (head gasket), black means running rich.

Red flag: smoke that keeps coming once the engine is warm.
Transmission feel

Foot on the brake, shift P-R-N-D. Each gear should engage in under a second with no clunk. On the drive, listen for shudder at 60-80 km/h.

Red flag: delay then a thud into Drive or Reverse.
Rust spots

Check wheel arches, rocker panels, door bottoms and under the trunk mat. On Ontario cars, surface rust is common - bubbling paint means it is deep.

Red flag: bubbling paint - it is always worse underneath.
AMBER = GOOD
Oil & coolant condition

Amber-brown oil is fine, black means neglected service, milky means coolant is mixing into the oil. Coolant should be bright, never rusty or oily.

Red flag: milky oil - walk away, that is a head gasket.

09 · QUESTIONS

Before you ask.

Is the VIN check really free?
Yes. The free check decodes any 17-character VIN and shows the vehicle's make, model, trim, engine, open safety recalls, an estimated market value and the make's known weak points. No sign-up, no card.
What does the full Drivee report include?
The full report ($19 CAD) covers the specific vehicle's accident and damage history, odometer readings with a rollback check, previous owners and use type, and theft records. It is delivered to your email, usually within a few hours. For $30 it also includes an Ontario lien check.
How do I check for a lien on a used car in Ontario?
Two ways: buy the Used Vehicle Information Package (UVIP, about $20) from ServiceOntario yourself, or order the Drivee $30 report which bundles the lien check with the full vehicle history in one document.
Can a VIN check detect odometer rollback?
The full report plots every recorded odometer reading over time. If a later reading is lower than an earlier one, that is a rollback flag. The sample report on this page shows what a clean, consistent mileage graph looks like.
Is Drivee affiliated with CARFAX?
No. Drivee is an independent Toronto-built service. Full reports are compiled from industry vehicle-history data and government sources, then delivered in a clear, plain-English format.
What should I check in person before buying a used car?
Cold start and steady idle near 750 rpm, fresh oil leaks, exhaust smoke colour, transmission engagement under one second, rust on arches and rockers, and fluid condition - amber oil is fine, milky oil means walk away. See the illustrated checklist above.
